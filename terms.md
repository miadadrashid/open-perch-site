## 4. Identification suggestions

The app's sound identification uses BirdNET, a machine-learning model from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz University of Technology.

**Its output is a ranked suggestion, not an identification.** It is wrong sometimes — confidently so. Accuracy varies with background noise, distance, wind, overlapping calls, region, and species. The app deliberately presents results as "possible matches" for this reason.

Do not rely on it as a sole source where accuracy matters: scientific recording, regulatory or permitting decisions, conservation reporting, or anything with legal or financial consequence. Verify against a field guide, a recording, or a person who knows.
